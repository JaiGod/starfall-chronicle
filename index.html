STARFALLCHRONICLE
An original pixel action-RPG · Made by Jaineil Rana (JaKing)

How to Play

Goal: recover the three Star Shards guarded in Whisperwood, the Ruins of Kharos and the Prism Deep. Follow the gold arrow.

  • WASD/Arrows move · Mouse aim
  • Left click/J attack - tap for combos, hold & release for your weapon's charged special
  • Space/Shift dodge-roll (invulnerable, costs stamina)
  • Right click/K cast active skill · F/1 potion
  • E talk / interact · I inventory · C character & skills · L quests · Esc pause · M mute
  • Touch: left side = joystick, buttons on the right. Attacks auto-aim at the nearest foe.

Weapons: Sword, Spear, Bow, Staff and Twin Daggers each have their own combo and special. Rarity runs Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary, with random enchantments (burn, frost, lifesteal, crit, swift, mighty).

Red zones are enemy attacks being telegraphed. Roll through them! Brann the smith upgrades (+1 to +10), reforges and fuses weapons. Drink from the town fountain to heal and save. The game also autosaves when you change areas.
